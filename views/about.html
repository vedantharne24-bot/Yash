ABOUT us

CodeCoast is a community-run hackathon that trades fluorescent conference halls for sea breeze, sunsets and 36 hours of building.

2022

Started as a weekend jam with 40 friends and one extension cord.

5

Seasons of meetups, workshops and build nights along the coast.

400+

Hackers have shipped projects with us — many for the very first time.

What we believe

Great software comes from curious people with room to play. We design CodeCoast so that first-timers feel welcome, veterans feel challenged, and everybody leaves with new friends and something they're proud of.

Who runs it

CodeCoast is organised by the CoastDev Collective — a volunteer crew of students, designers and engineers. Every rupee from sponsors and merch goes back into prizes, food and the next season.

Get involved

Want to mentor, judge, volunteer or sponsor? Drop us a message or check out the sponsorship tiers. Hackers — applications are open.
