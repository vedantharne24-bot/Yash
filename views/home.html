THE VISION

CodeCoast is a creative uprising. We are building a haven where the walls between code, art, and innovation melt away. For 36 hours, we call on the builders and the rule-breakers to enter a space where every commit shapes tomorrow. Here your terminal turns into a sketchbook, and the screen into your stage.

The Coast's First & Finest Seaside Hackathon.

CodeCoast is a hackathon built by a community of makers, designers and engineers who believe the best ideas show up when you change the scenery. Every season gathers curious minds to ship technology with purpose and impact.
