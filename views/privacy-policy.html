PRIVACY policy

Last updated: March 2027. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

When you apply, join the referral program, order merch or contact us, we store the details you type into the form: names, email addresses, phone numbers, college/organisation, and the content of your message or application.

How we use it

  • To review applications and contact shortlisted teams.
  • To calculate referral points and show a leaderboard (first name, last initial and college only).
  • To process and hand over merch orders.
  • To reply to your enquiries.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data. Sponsors only receive participant details when you explicitly opt in.

Cookies & storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your cart and whether you dismissed the referral popup. No tracking cookies are used.

Your rights

Email hello@codecoast.dev to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you.
